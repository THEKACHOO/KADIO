About Kachoo

The Kachoo is a digital media and news company that also operates in the procurement of digital features. The head office of The Kachoo is located at Jalan Jenderal Sudirman, Lots 61-62, South Jakarta, Indonesia.

The Kachoo has several projects, one of which is Kadio. It is Kadio that collaborates with several companies that align with the concepts of broadcasting, cinematography, and/or studio production.

In this context, Phochio° is a collaborative project between The Kachoo and Studio 35°. The Kachoo provides website or digital platforms for studio use, offers digital printing sessions, and facilitates partnerships with business entities related to this project.
